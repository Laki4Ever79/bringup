## Design
- Pocket-sized battery-powered climate logger with BLE phone access, USB-C charging and native USB-C firmware flashing using the ESP32-C3's USB pins, onboard storage of temperature/humidity history in the ESP32-C3's internal 4 MB flash, an MCU-driven status LED that blinks on each reading, and a dedicated hardware charge LED lit while charging from the charger's active-low CHRG output through a resistor.
- Board 40×30 mm; connectors: J1 usb-c on top, SW1 button on right
- Parts: U1 ESP32-C3-MINI-1 (C2838502), U2 SHTC3-TR-2.5KS (C194656), U3 XC6206P332MR-G (C5446), U4 TP4054-42-SOT25R (C32574), J1 TYPE-C-31-M-12 (C165948), J2 S2B-PH-K-S(LF)(SN) (C173752), D1 KT-0603R (C2286), D2 KT-0603R (C2286), R1 0402WGF1001TCE (C11702), R2 0402WGF1001TCE (C11702), SW1 TS-1187A-B-A-B (C318884)

## Brief review

Verdict: fail after 9 reviews (at most 2 fix rounds).
- Review 1 of revision 11: fail
  - blocker (requirements, rails, parts, behavior): The 6-month battery-life requirement is not plausibly met with the chosen charger. U4 is a TP4054 linear charger, whose BAT pin remains connected to the battery and typically draws standby current from the cell when USB is absent. The provided power budget only counts VBAT 0.02 mA + 3V3 0.084 mA + LDO IQ 0.001 mA = 0.105 mA average, giving about 198 days, but it omits the charger’s battery-side current. Even a modest additional ~50 µA would raise total average to ~0.155 mA and reduce life to 500/0.155/24 ≈ 134 days, below the 180-day target. Because the brief hinges on long unattended battery operation, leaving out this always-connected load clearly undermines the requirement. Suggestion: Use a charger with explicitly low battery leakage / power-path behavior suitable for long-lived battery products, or include the TP4054 battery leakage/standby current in the budget and show the design still exceeds 180 days under worst-case expected conditions.
  - major (requirements, behavior, parts): History storage is only summarized, not actually allocated in the contract. The brief requires that a phone app can download stored history, and the design relies on the ESP32-C3 module’s internal 4 MB flash for both firmware and logged data, but the contract gives no retention target, sample format, or flash partition/storage allocation proving useful history can be stored for the intended every-few-days readout pattern. Suggestion: State the intended logging depth and reserve storage accordingly in the design/firmware behavior so it is clear the onboard flash can hold an adequate history alongside firmware.
  - minor (requirements, parts, pins, behavior): The brief asks for 'a status LED that blinks briefly on each reading and shows charging state.' The design includes two LEDs, with only D1 explicitly pinned as STATUS_LED, while charging indication appears to rely on a separate D2 via the charger's CHRG output but is not described in pins/behavior. This likely meets the user need, but the contract does not make the charge indication path explicit. Suggestion: Document the charge-indication wiring/behavior explicitly so it is unambiguous how charging state is shown.
