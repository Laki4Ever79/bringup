## Design
- Pocket-sized battery-powered climate logger with BLE phone access, USB-C charging/programming, onboard status indication, and on-module history storage for temperature and humidity readings. Retains reading history in on-module flash for the intended use case of checking every few days, with a target of longer-than-30-day retention at 5-minute intervals if flash budget allows.
- Board 40×30 mm; connectors: J1 usb-c on bottom, SW1 button on right
- Parts: U1 ESP32-C3-MINI-1 (C2838502), U2 SHTC3-TR-2.5KS (C194656), U3 XC6206P332MR-G (C5446), U4 TP4054-42-SOT25R (C32574), J1 TYPE-C-31-M-12 (C165948), J2 S2B-PH-K-S(LF)(SN) (C173752), D1 KT-0603R (C2286), D2 KT-0603R (C2286), R1 0402WGF1001TCE (C11702), R2 0402WGF1001TCE (C11702), SW1 TS-1187A-B-A-B (C318884)
- Pins: I2C_SCL=GPIO4→U2.SCL, I2C_SDA=GPIO5→U2.SDA, STATUS_LED=GPIO8→D1.K, BOOT_BUTTON=GPIO9→SW1.A, USB_DP=GPIO19→J1.DP, USB_DN=GPIO18→J1.DN
- Rails (peak/rated): VBUS 0/500 mA, average 0 mA; VBAT 250/500 mA, average 0.001 mA; 3V3 90/600 mA, average 0.051 mA
- Estimated battery life: 393 days vs target 180 days

  - blocker (requirements, rails, behavior): The 6-month battery-life requirement is not met by the stated duty cycle and worst-case active current assumptions used by the deterministic check. Firmware wakes every 300 s for 300 ms, and the computed check uses the busiest battery-fed rail peak of 500 mA for that whole wake: 0.3 s / 300 s × 500 mA = 0.5 mA average while awake, plus 9 µA sleep/regulator, for about 0.509 mA total.
